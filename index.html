              Twos for acting. A hold of at least 12 frames. One or two smear frames in the sakuga shot.
            

            

              Camera
              Where the cut is, where the eyes sit, and how a tall frame is composed.

              The HyperFrames file. A color dip is the cut. Lint has to pass.
